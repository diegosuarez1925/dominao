
# Application Pitch

We are introducing **DOMINAO**, an application for the players and fans of Puerto Rican Dominos. Though the general concept of "a Domino game" is very popular and widespread, the Puerto Rican variant of the game has seen signifcantly less representation. Players, as of today, need to meet in person to be able to run matches against their peers, making the accesibility of the game more limited. In this search for solving the Puerto Rican Dominos accesibility issue is where **DOMINAO** steps in. It offers *Private Tables* where friends can run synchronous games in real time, just like games in real life happen. To further make the game more accesible to everyone, it also offers *Public Matchmaking*, where players get paired randomly at any time. Even with private tables, players would still need to agree on times to hop in and play: with public mathcmaking, anyone can hope into a quick Domino match anytime, anywhere. And, to make it all as simple as it can be, **Dominao** offers *Guest Sessions*, where players do not need to create and sign into accounts to be able to enjoy the features of the app. This makes it even simpler to hop in and play whenever, further lowering the bar of entry for the game.
