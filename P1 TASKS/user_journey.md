# User Journey

To tell the story of a stakeholder, I will repeat, one last time, the story of my grandfather, and how his need triggered the creation of this app.

My grandpa has always loved playing Dominos. When he was diagnosed with skin cancer and started chemotherapy, however, he needed to stay multiple times a month by himself receiving treatment for multiple hours at a time. He grew bored of staying there doing nothing, and wanted to play Dominos - his favorite gane - to cure that boredom. Usually, that would require him.bringing a table, a domino set, and three friends over to his sessioms, which was infeasible, so he thought "maybe there's some way to play online!". He downloaded a couple of apps that seemed to be close to what he was looking for, but no app was there that used the ruleset he was used to: The Puerto Rican variant. That is, until his grandson coded **Dominao!** for him.

As soon as he searched it up, he was prompted to enter a display name or to login. It was awfully convenient: he didnt need to provide any emails, phone numbers, or write down complicated passwords for him to remember later. He was immidiately brought into the home page of the app, with a big button that said "Play!", and so he pressed it. After a little waiting screen, he was brought into a new screen where he could see three other players' face down hands, as well as his face up hand, with the game board displayed in the middle. The game emmulated the rules of PR Dominos perfectly, and he did not need to arrange for a whole afternoon for people to meet up to play to make this happen. Safe to say he did not get bored during chemos again...

He eventually wanted to change it up, as he had been playing with random teammates against random opponents every game, and he missed the fun that comes from playing around the playstyles of people you know. So, he called 3 of his brothers, and told them all to look up the app. Again, none of them needed yo sign in. My grandfather created a private table, and sent the join code over to his brothers, and, after putting the codes in, they started running a game of 4, even when no one eas in person with the other. 

Thanks to the app, my grandfather was able to fire up games wherever he was, at whatever time he would like. What before took multiple calls and time allocation to arrange, now took just a couple button presses. He is now able to play his favorite game as he has known it from childhood at any time and with ease.
